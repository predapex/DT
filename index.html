Member / connection / footing checks
This is a conservative scenario screen, not a failure prediction or permission to climb. Brace and handrail screen values are vertical-only idealizations; swing-load member forces and actual capacities are not calculated. The upper support elevation is a drawing placeholder, not a surveyed coordinate. Swing moments are direction-swept; footing reactions and loaded-leg demand are not calculated because required stiffness, connection, and anchor data are absent. Red means an unverified legacy screen is exceeded; purple means demand or capacity is unverified; grey means below an unverified screen, not safe. No green safety rating is used. The ⅓ deterioration loss is your assumed allowance, not a measured condition assessment. See the point checks below.
Secondary tower load distribution
Purple arrows trace the secondary load paths after the gallows forces enter the tower at the lower knee-brace connection, boom/handrail connection, and upper angle/roof-frame connection. They show transfer into the existing framing, neighboring legs and foundations. Arrow thickness responds to the total load including both ropes. No beneficial load sharing is credited in the member checks. This is a load-path visualization, not a finite-element calculation of the exact force in each individual brace.
Rear footing uplift screen
Swing-direction overturning moments are shown above. Individual footing uplift/compression reactions are not calculated because tower stiffness, guy-cable behavior, footing weight, anchor/rebar resistance and soil restraint are not modeled. Treat the shown moments as demand envelopes, not footing reactions.
Attachment / member assumptions
CORNER MOUNT: gallows is attached at one tower corner/leg, not at the face centerline · Loaded gallows leg ONLY → 3×3×3/16 in square tube; other three tower legs remain 3×3×3/16 angle · Bottom brace attaches to the loaded square-tube leg · Top beam → 2×2×3/16 in angle handrail · Upper support → 2×2×3/16 in angle iron attached to roof frame · Gallows → 2×2×1/4 in angle · Bolts → 1/2 in high-tensile · Rope reach → 99.5 in · Lower brace → ~12 ft · Lower brace angle → calculated from the displayed geometry
Screening values depend heavily on actual unsupported span, connection geometry, bolt grade/layout, welds, eccentricity, corrosion and anchorage. A structural engineer should replace these estimates with measured capacities before anyone climbs on it.
